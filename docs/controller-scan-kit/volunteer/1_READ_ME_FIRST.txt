HELP AMJAD WITH YOUR CONTROLLER - START HERE

You only need a Windows 10 or 11 computer and your controller.
You do not need Python, programming knowledge, or another program.

1. Right-click the ZIP Amjad sent you and choose "Extract All".
   Click "Extract", then open the extracted folder.
   Do not run the tool from inside the ZIP.

2. Close your game and Steam. Leave your controller in the mode you
   normally use. Do not change its settings for this first test.

3. Double-click "2_RUN_ME.bat" in this folder.
   Windows may hide ".bat", so you may see only "2_RUN_ME".

4. A text window opens. Type y, then press ENTER to begin.
   Choose the model written on your controller or its box.
   For X05, X05 Pro, X10, D10, etc., choose "Other model" and type its name.

5. Choose how you normally connect it: cable, little USB receiver, or
   Bluetooth. The window tells you exactly what to unplug and reconnect.
   Press ENTER after doing each instruction. Leave your mouse and keyboard
   plugged in. If you do not recognize a device, do not guess; skip it.

6. Read the short result summary. If it looks okay to share, type y and
   press ENTER. The tool saves a ZIP and opens the "results" folder.

7. Open "3_SEND_THE_RESULT.txt" for the last step.

The tool reads device information. It does not change controller settings
or send anything automatically. You can stop at any time with CTRL+C.
There are no button tests or extra Bluetooth tests in this first run.

If something is confusing or blocked, stop and read
"IF_SOMETHING_GOES_WRONG.txt". You do not need to solve it yourself.
