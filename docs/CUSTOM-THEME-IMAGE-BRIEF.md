# X20CTL custom themes: image brief (index)

The brief is now a set of files in `docs/theme-image-brief/`. Give ChatGPT **one style at a time**, in a fresh chat.

## How to run a style

1. Open a new ChatGPT chat with image generation.
2. Paste `00-MASTER.md`. It replies "Master received."
3. Attach your current X20CTL logo as a reference image, then paste the style file. It replies "Style received."
4. Say **start**. It makes the 3 logo files first. Approve or give notes.
5. It then makes wallpapers 01 to 18, one per reply, with a contact sheet after 06, 12 and 18.
6. Reject anything with stray text, a figure, a controller, or a busy centre.

## The seven styles

| File | Style | One line |
|---|---|---|
| `01-vaporwave.md` | Vaporwave | Dreamy pastel pink and purple, retro surrealism |
| `02-frutiger-aero.md` | Frutiger Aero | Glossy glass, water, sky blue, Y2K, in a blue-hour version for dark UI |
| `03-dark-fantasy-gothic.md` | Dark Fantasy / Gothic | Crimson, black, ornamental cathedral architecture |
| `04-manga.md` | Manga / Ink | Fine ink, halftones, white on black paper, one red |
| `05-tokyo-night.md` | Tokyo Night | Elegant everyday dark interface, rainy night city |
| `06-synthwave.md` | Synthwave | Colourful retro arcade identity, neon highway |
| `07-crimson-rebellion.md` | Crimson Rebellion | Bold comic-style redesign, torn paper, thick ink |

Removed from the first draft: Glassmorphism and AMOLED Black.

## What is in each file

- Identity, exact hex palette with roles, lighting, materials, texture, perspective, level of detail
- A signature continuity element that must appear on every page
- Recurring motifs, and a banned list
- Logo spec with three paste-ready prompts (full, mark, mono)
- 18 paste-ready wallpaper descriptions, one per page, with what sits on top of each
- A consistency checklist

Total: 7 styles x (3 logo files + 18 wallpapers) = 147 images.

## Notes for me, not for ChatGPT

- Image generators often ignore "no text" and "calm centre". Check each wallpaper at full size.
- Manga, Gothic and Crimson Rebellion all use red. They are written to stay distinct (fine ink halftone, painterly architecture, thick flat collage). Compare their contact sheets side by side.
- Frutiger Aero is the blue-hour version on purpose. Do not accept a bright midday sky.
- These are AI-generated. Keep the prompts and the originals, and check the generator's terms before shipping them inside the app.
- The app currently ships six themes: Default, Nexus, Dusk, Hollow, Nocturne, Sunrise. These styles are new directions, not part of the app until we agree which become real themes.
