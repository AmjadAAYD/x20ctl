# Intro Audio Provenance

## Windows opening film (`intro.mp4`)

`desktop-dotnet/X20Ctl.Product/Brand/intro.mp4` (`1920x1080`, `60 fps`, `7.1 s`) opens the Windows app (Draft 7 picture). Its stereo AAC track (`-19.2 LUFS` integrated, `-4.3 dBFS` sample peak) is synthetically generated by [`tools/build_intro_sound_strings.py`](../../../tools/build_intro_sound_strings.py) using sine and saw partials in E with a shared room reverb and low piano strike at `4.03 s` — no third-party recordings, kits, or samples.

Audio is muted automatically with reduced motion, in UI review fixtures, or when **Tools -> Opening intro -> Sound** is toggled off.

## Linux Studio (`X20Ctl.Linux`)

Starting in V0.025 (and enforced by `X20Ctl.Linux.Review --input-ownership`), **Linux Studio is intentionally silent** and packages no audio files or sound playback classes (`intro-film.wav` and the superseded `tools/build_intro_sound.py` script were removed during repository cleanup).
